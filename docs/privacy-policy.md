# Children Books Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

Policy URL: https://childrenbooksprivacypolicy.blogspot.com/2026/07/children-books-privacy-policy-effective.html

Children Books (`com.childrenbooks`) is a kids learning app for reading, listening, and interactive learning activities.

## Data Collection

Children Books does not ask children to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone recordings, or payment information.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social login SDKs in the current release build.

## Device Features

The app uses local app content, bundled media, and the device Text-to-Speech engine to pronounce learning words and sounds. Text-to-Speech is handled by the speech engine installed on the user's device.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage permissions.

## External Links And Parent Check

Some actions can open content outside the app, such as the full privacy policy, Google Play rating page, or sharing. These actions are protected by a parent check before leaving the app.

## Children And Families

Children Books is designed for children and family use. The app is intended to provide child-friendly educational content and does not show third-party ads.

Parents or guardians can contact the developer with privacy questions or requests.
